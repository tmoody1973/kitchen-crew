# 005 — A confirmed receipt's total replaces the paid boxes it covers

**Decision** — After "Done shopping", the tally counts the receipt's total (register prices, tax, fees) instead of the paid boxes for the rows that receipt covers; Claude reads the receipt photo, but a person confirms every number before it counts.

**Why this came up** — Tarik wanted to finish a shop, scan the receipt, and track actual against planned by week, month and quarter. Paid boxes miss tax, bag and delivery fees, and register surprises, so the weekly tally was always a little low. Counting both the boxes and the receipt would count the trip twice.

**Options**
1. **Receipt total replaces the covered boxes (chosen).** The tally is what actually left the bank account. Cost: Dinners/Extras chips still add up paid boxes, so a "Receipt +$X" chip shows the difference.
2. **Keep the tally on paid boxes; show the receipt beside it.** Simpler, never double-counts. Cost: the main number is never the real spend.
3. **Read every line item off the receipt and match it to rows.** Most detailed. Cost: line matching is error-prone (abbreviations like "SPAGHETTI BARILLA") and hours more work on submission day.

**What we chose and why** — Option 1. Tarik picked "receipt total" from the two tally options; Claude recommended it and proposed totals-only reading (option 3 deferred) to fit the deadline.

**What we gave up** — No per-item check against the receipt; a misread total is only caught by the person confirming it. Editing a paid box after confirming doesn't change that trip's total. The demo's monthly and quarterly views rely on 12 made-up sample weeks, labelled "Sample" on screen.

**How we'll know if this was right** — After a real shop, the tally matches the bank charge to the cent, and Tarik rarely has to correct what the crew read.
