# Mise app shell (all screens)

Mode: Operate. The visitor completes tasks: set a budget, plan dinners in chat, shop with a live tally.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The tally is a farmers-market price board, and every price in the app is a shelf tag. Refuses the category default: a green fintech dashboard with charts and soft cards, and its opposite, a cream recipe blog.

OWN-WORLD: Slate chalkboard (#1F2A24) with chalk-white numerals in chunky cells, held in a crate-wood frame (#6B4F2E); yellow shelf tags (#FFD23F) with a hard ink drop shadow, tilted; tomato (#E5402B) chunky press buttons with a darker bottom lip; kale green (#2E8B3E) means under, squash orange (#F26B1D) means over, always with words; stamped ink outlines for verdicts ("FITS YOUR BUDGET ✓"); off-white produce-bin ground (#FBF8F1); Bowlby One for signs and numbers, Figtree for everything you read.

STORY: You see where the week stands in one glance, tap a price box, type what you paid, and watch the board flip and the row turn green or orange with a sentence saying which.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On a 390px phone, the slate board sits at the top of List and Plan (spent of budget in board cells, then left and days left, then the Dinners/Extras split as dashed chalk pills, then the verdict line in tag yellow); below it the store picker and the first rows with tilted tags and big paid boxes within thumb reach. Primary action: the paid box on each row; on Plan, the chat input fixed at the bottom.

FORM: Farmers-market chalkboard and crate stamps; position 5 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key 9e161d8a. Raises: numerals flip cell by cell when the tally changes (from split-flap board); one strict color legend where green and orange only ever mean under and over (from transit map).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
